Gabriel Costello runs half of the city's docks and trusts almost no one. The one person he never thought to question is the man sitting at his right hand every Sunday dinner — and the one person he never learned to listen to is his own mother, who lost her hearing when he was a boy.

When Chloe Bennett, a quiet waitress hired for a single evening, notices the old woman's trembling hands spelling out the same three words again and again, she has to decide whether a warning from a stranger is worth risking her life for.

A story about a language no one bothered to learn, a betrayal hidden in plain sight, and the people we overlook until it is almost too late.
