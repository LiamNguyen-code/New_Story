Lieutenant Commander Elena Hale came home for her father's sixtieth birthday expecting the usual: polite questions from his golf friends and the familiar silence from him. She did not expect him to raise a glass and joke that his daughter "drives boats for the government."

What Richard Hale doesn't know is why his daughter keeps checking her phone — or who is about to call her name.
