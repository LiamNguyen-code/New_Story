Maya Torres works the closing shift at a 24-hour pharmacy and walks to her car alone at two in the morning. For twenty-three nights, a scarred gray stray waits at the edge of the lot, and for twenty-three nights she leaves him half of her dinner.

On the twenty-fourth night, the dog refuses to let her open her car door.

A short, tense and heartwarming story about kindness that comes back when you need it most.
