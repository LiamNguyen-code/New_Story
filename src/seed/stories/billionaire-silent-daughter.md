Since the accident, Lily Ashford has not said a single word. Her father, Nathan, has flown in specialists from three countries. Every one of them says the same thing: give her time.

Then Rosa arrives — a nanny with a secondhand coat, a deaf younger brother, and a habit of watching hands instead of faces. On her first afternoon, she notices Lily's fingers moving under the table, spelling the same name over and over.
